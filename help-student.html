How to use this website

Joining your class (first time only)

  1. Go to the student page and click Register.
  2. Type the class code your teacher gives you.
  3. Type your first name and last name.
  4. Make up a password (at least 6 characters). Don't use a password you use anywhere else.
  5. Click Register.

Logging in

Click Log in and type your class code, your first and last name, and your password. Then click Log in.

When you're finished, click Sign out in the top right, especially on a shared computer.

Lessons and challenges

After you log in you'll see your lessons. Your teacher unlocks new ones in class.

  1. Click Open challenge.
  2. Read the challenge and work out the secret answer (the flag).
  3. Type it in the answer box and click Check answer.

If it's right, you'll see Correct! and the lesson gets a Solved badge. If not, have another look and try again. Answers aren't case sensitive, so "HACKER" and "hacker" are the same.

Live CTF (competitions)

Sometimes your teacher runs a Live CTF. It's a race to solve challenges, with a leaderboard on the big screen.

  1. A pink Join the Live CTF button appears on your lessons page. Click it.
  2. If you're playing in teams, you might get to pick a team and choose your team's name and colour. Keep it friendly!
  3. Wait for your teacher to press START.
  4. The challenge shows up on your screen. Type the flag in the box at the top and click Submit.
  5. Got it right? You'll see your points, your place, and how far behind the next team you are.

Points

Each challenge has its own timer. The faster you solve it, the more points you get (up to 1000). Even a slow answer gets at least 500 points, so never give up! If you're slow on one challenge, you can catch up by being fast on the next.

Stuck?

In some games there's a Skip button. Skipping gives you 0 points for that challenge and you can't go back to it, so only skip if you're really stuck.

Teams

Anyone in your team can type the answer. When someone solves it, the whole team gets the points and moves on together.

Ask your teacher if...

  • You forgot your password. Your teacher can give you a new one.
  • Your name is spelt wrong, or you want to change it.
  • You want to change your password.
  • You can't find a lesson, or you're in the wrong class.
  • You're in the wrong team in a Live CTF.
