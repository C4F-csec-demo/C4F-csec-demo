The Canteen

Hillview High put their canteen orders online over the holidays. The ordering button is switched off until Monday, and the price of your order is worked out before you ever press it.

Both of those things happen on your laptop.

Mission: the canteen will hand over a word if it ever ends up owing you money. Work out how to make that happen.

Stuck? Read this

Right click the Place order button and choose Inspect. There is a word in there stopping you clicking it, and a box just above that the canteen is using to remember what you owe.

Then think about what a price is allowed to be, and what the canteen never bothered to check.
