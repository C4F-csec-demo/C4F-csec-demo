Remember Me

Websites are forgetful. Every time you click something, the site has basically never met you before. So when you arrive, it hands you a wristband and asks you to hold onto it.

This water park checked your wristband and decided you are not a member. Your wristband is in your pocket, not theirs.

Mission: get in. You are not changing anything on their computer, only what is written on your own wristband.

Stuck? Read this

Open DevTools and find the Application tab (you may need the » arrow to see it). Click Cookies on the left, then the name of this site. Double click a value to change it.

One more thing: changing it does nothing on its own. The water park only looks at your wristband when you walk in, so you have to reload the page.

Members only. Sorry!
