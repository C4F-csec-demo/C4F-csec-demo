Crack the Caesar Cipher

You have intercepted a secret message. It was scrambled with a Caesar cipher, which shifts every letter along the alphabet by the same number of places.

The message says:

YMJ XJHWJY BTWI NX MFHPJW

You do not know the key. Can you find it?

Hint: try shifting the shortest word backwards by 1, then 2, then 3, until a real word appears. Keep a list of the shifts you have tried.

To decode, move each letter backwards along the alphabet. If you go past A, wrap around to Z.
