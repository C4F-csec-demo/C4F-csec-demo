Beanbag Juice Bar

Buy ten smoothies, get one free. The juice bar does not keep your card behind the counter. It asks your browser to hold it for them.

Keep pressing the button and you will notice something: the card never quite fills up.

Mission: claim the free smoothie.

Stuck? Read this

Open DevTools, go to the Application tab, click Cookies on the left and then the name of this site. Your card is in there as a number.

Change it, then reload. The juice bar only reads your card when you walk in.
